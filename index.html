What we do

End-to-end cabling & jointing services

From the first trench to the final insulation test, our certified team handles every stage of your power cable installation.

HV Cable Jointing

Straight-through, transition and branch joints for 6.6kV and 22kV networks.

  • XLPE & PILC cables
  • Heat-shrink & cold-shrink systems
  • Transition joints (PILC–XLPE)

Cable Terminations

Indoor and outdoor terminations for switchgear, transformers and RMUs.

  • Separable & elbow connectors
  • Stress-control terminations
  • Lugging & crimping

LV Cabling & Jointing

Low-voltage power distribution cabling for buildings, plants and infrastructure.

  • Armoured & unarmoured cables
  • Resin & mechanical joints
  • Main & sub-main feeders

Cable Laying & Pulling

Professional installation in trenches, ducts, trays, ladders and risers.

  • Direct burial & duct pulling
  • Tray & ladder dressing
  • Cable route planning

Testing & Commissioning

Verify every installation with documented, standards-based testing.

  • Insulation resistance (IR) tests
  • VLF / HiPot withstand tests
  • Continuity & phasing checks

Fault Repair & Maintenance

Rapid response to cable faults with repair, replacement and preventive upkeep.

  • 24/7 emergency call-out
  • Fault location support
  • Scheduled maintenance

FAQ

Common questions

What voltage levels do you work on?

We handle low-voltage (230/400V) cabling and jointing as well as medium and high-voltage work at 6.6kV and 22kV, including XLPE and legacy PILC cables.

Do you provide emergency cable fault repair?

Yes. Our team is on call 24/7 for cable faults. Call our hotline and we'll mobilise a crew to restore your supply as quickly and safely as possible.

Can you work during planned shutdowns at night or on weekends?

Absolutely. Most jointing work happens during scheduled shutdowns. We regularly work nights, weekends and public holidays to minimise disruption to your operations.

Do you supply the jointing kits and materials?

We can supply approved joint and termination kits from established manufacturers, or install client-supplied materials. We'll advise the right system for your cable and application.

Will I receive test reports after the job?

Yes. Every job includes a handover pack with joint records, insulation resistance and withstand test results, and photographs of each stage.
